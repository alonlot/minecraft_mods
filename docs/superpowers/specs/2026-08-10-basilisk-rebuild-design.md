# Basilisk rebuild — design

Date: 2026-08-10

A full rebuild of `hogwartsmagic:basilisk`. The existing entity is an eight-cube
hand-written model wiggled by sine waves, three sounds, and an instant-kill
gaze. This replaces all of it: a 14-block Blockbench serpent on a
path-following spine, keyframe action clips, eleven generated sounds, and a
charged stare the player can survive if they react.

## Approach

Three rigs were considered:

- **Pure keyframe** (the Giant's pattern) reads as a stiff rod when a long body
  turns a corner.
- **Pure procedural** (what exists today) cannot express a dramatic strike or a
  burrow.
- **Hybrid** — chosen. The spine follows the path the head actually took; the
  head, jaw, tongue and hood are driven by authored clips layered on top.

## Geometry

`blockbench_models/basilisk.bbmodel`, Modded Entity format, exported through the
Modded Entity codec into `BasiliskModel`. Built 1:1 — 224 px nose to tail, 14
blocks, renderer scale `1.0`. Texture 512×256.

- **`head`** (nested, keyframed): elongated wedge skull, heavy brow ridge, a
  bony crest of spines over the crown, a hinged `jaw`, four `fang` cubes, a
  forked `tongue`, and separate `eye_left` / `eye_right` cubes rendered on an
  emissive layer so the yellow eyes burn in the dark.
- **`hood`**: flared neck plates that spread during the rear-up.
- **`seg01`–`seg13`**: spine segments, siblings under `root` rather than nested,
  each positioned absolutely in local space every frame. Girth is thick through
  segments 3–7 and tapers to a whip tail.

Dark bottle-green keeled dorsal scales, pale yellow-cream ventral scutes,
blood-orange gums, ivory fangs. Texture generated by
`build_scripts/generate_basilisk_texture.py`.

## Spine solver

`BasiliskEntity` keeps a 128-entry ring buffer of `(position, yBodyRot)`,
recorded every tick on both sides — positions are synced, so client and server
agree. `BasiliskModel` samples the buffer at an increasing lag per segment and
converts each sample into a local-space offset plus yaw and pitch. A freshly
spawned basilisk with an empty buffer falls back to a straight line under a sine
wave. Per-state `curl` and `lift` multipliers let actions override the path
follow for the coil, the rear-up and the burrow.

## States

A server-authoritative synched byte with a state tick, following
`GiantEntity`'s shape. Movement locks during every committed state. Clip
selection in `setupAnim` uses if/else on the state ordinal, never a `switch` on
the enum — the synthetic switch-map helper has been observed to fail to load in
this mod's dev environment.

| State | Ticks | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `IDLE` / `SLITHER` | — | Path follow plus a speed-scaled S-wave, tongue flicks, head sway |
| `REAR` | 30 | Front five segments lift into a vertical S, hood flares, jaw gapes, sustained hiss. Telegraph only. |
| `STRIKE` | 14 | Head lances forward at t=6. 100° front cone, 7.0 reach, venomous bite. |
| `SPIT` | 22 | Head jerks at t=10 and launches a `BasiliskVenomEntity` glob on an arc. |
| `TAIL_SWEEP` | 24 | Front coils, the tail whips a full arc, heavy knockback. |
| `BURROW_DOWN` | 26 | Head dives; segments hide progressively front to back. Dirt burst. |
| `BURROW_UP` | 22 | Erupts beneath the target; segments unhide back to front and launch victims. |
| `BLINDED` | latch | Head weaves side to side searching. Gaze disabled. |
| `DEATH` | 60 | Writhing collapse, coils going slack from head to tail. |

## The charged gaze

A per-player charge counter runs 0 → 50 ticks on the server. It rises only when
all of the following hold: the player is within 24 blocks, mutual line of sight,
the basilisk's head is aimed at the player, and the player's crosshair is within
about 12° of the basilisk's head specifically. It decays at twice that rate the
moment any condition breaks.

Client feedback rides on a new `basilisk_gaze` mob effect whose amplitude
carries the charge tier, 0 to 4. No custom packet is involved, so it behaves on
a dedicated server.

- Tier 0–1: faint grey desaturation at the screen edges, a low sub-bass swell.
- Tier 2–3: a stone-crackle texture creeps inward, the heartbeat rises in pitch.
- Tier 4: the vignette closes, the screen greys out, then petrification and
  death.

The player escapes by looking away, breaking line of sight, or blinding the
basilisk. The gaze cannot charge while the basilisk is blinded or burrowed.

## Blinded state

Damage landed while the attacker stands in the basilisk's front 60° cone
accumulates against a head-hitzone counter. Past 60 damage a permanent latch
trips for that individual: the eye cubes swap to a gouged texture on a second
render layer, the gaze goes dead, and targeting switches to hunting by sound —
the nearest entity that recently moved or attacked, rather than the nearest
visible one. Sprinting nearby becomes dangerous, sneaking becomes viable.

The latch is permanent by design. Blinding a basilisk is a real reward for a
real risk.

## Sounds

Eleven clips through the ElevenLabs SFX API, converted to mono OGG at 44.1 kHz
into `assets/hogwartsmagic/sounds/basilisk/`, generated by
`build_scripts/generate_basilisk_sounds.py`:

`ambient1` (deep slow hiss), `ambient2` (scales dragging over stone),
`hiss_threat`, `strike`, `spit`, `bite`, `burrow`, `erupt`, `gaze_heartbeat`,
`hurt`, `death`.

## Stats and spawning

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Hitbox | 3.5 W × 3.0 H |
| Max health | 300 |
| Movement speed | 0.30 |
| Attack damage | 18 |
| Follow range | 48 |
| Knockback resistance | 0.95 |

The hitbox sits on the front body. The back two-thirds of the serpent trails
outside it and is decorative — it cannot be hit and does not block movement.
This is unavoidable for a body this long under an axis-aligned box, and matches
what the Ender Dragon does. The render bounding box is inflated to 20 blocks so
the tail never culls.

Spawn egg only, as today. No natural spawns.

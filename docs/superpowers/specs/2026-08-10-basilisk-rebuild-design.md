# Basilisk rebuild — design

Date: 2026-08-10

A full rebuild of `hogwartsmagic:basilisk`. The existing entity is an eight-cube
hand-written model wiggled by sine waves, three sounds, and an instant-kill
gaze. This replaces all of it: a 14-block Blockbench serpent on a
path-following spine, keyframe action clips, eleven generated sounds, and a
charged stare the player can survive if they react.

## Approach

Three rigs were considered:

- **Pure keyframe** (the Giant's pattern) reads as a stiff rod when a long body
  turns a corner.
- **Pure procedural** (what exists today) cannot express a dramatic strike or a
  burrow.
- **Hybrid** — chosen. The spine follows the path the head actually took; the
  head, jaw, tongue and hood are driven by authored clips layered on top.

## Geometry

`blockbench_models/basilisk.bbmodel`, Modded Entity format, exported through the
Modded Entity codec into `BasiliskModel`. Built 1:1 — 249 px nose to tail, so
about 15½ blocks, renderer scale `1.0`. Texture 512×128.

- **`head`** (nested, keyframed): a wedge skull that steps down 22 → 17 → 11 → 7
  px wide from the venom glands to the nose, separate scowling brow ridges over
  each eye rather than one shelf, a low dorsal crest and two backswept horns, a
  hinged `jaw`, four `fang` cubes tucked inside the closed jaw so they are
  revealed only when it gapes, a forked `tongue`, and separate `eye_left` /
  `eye_right` cubes rendered on an emissive layer so the yellow slit eyes burn
  in the dark.

- **`hood_left` / `hood_right`**: neck plates that spread during the rear-up.
- **`seg01`–`seg13`**: spine segments, siblings under `root` rather than nested,
  each positioned absolutely in local space every frame. Girth swells from 13 px
  at the neck to 22 px through segments 4 and 5, then tapers to a 3 px whip tail.

The skin is deliberately near-black bottle green over dull bone scutes, with a
dark maroon mouth: an earlier pass in brighter green over an even scale grid
read as a toy rather than a monster. Dorsal scales carry low-frequency saddle
bands that run continuously from the skull to the tail tip. Texture generated by
`build_scripts/generate_basilisk_texture.py`, which also emits the emissive eye
overlay.

## Spine solver

`BasiliskEntity` keeps a 160-entry ring buffer of `(position, yBodyRot)`,
recorded every tick on both sides — positions are synced, so client and server
agree. `BasiliskModel` walks it back by **arc length**, not by time, so the body
neither bunches up when the basilisk slows nor stretches when it charges; each
sample becomes a local-space offset plus a yaw and pitch aiming the segment at
the next sample.

When the recorded path runs out — a fresh spawn, or a long spell of standing
still — the remainder extends in a straight line along the body yaw *recorded at
that point* rather than the current one, which is what lets the tail lag behind
a turn in place. Per-state lift and coil terms then override the path for the
rear-up, the tail sweep and the burrow.

## States

A server-authoritative synched byte with a state tick, following
`GiantEntity`'s shape. Movement locks during every committed state. Clip
selection in `setupAnim` uses if/else on the state ordinal, never a `switch` on
the enum — the synthetic switch-map helper has been observed to fail to load in
this mod's dev environment.

| State | Ticks | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `IDLE` / `SLITHER` | — | Path follow plus a speed-scaled S-wave, tongue flicks, head sway |
| `REAR` | 30 | Front five segments lift into a vertical S, hood flares, jaw gapes, sustained hiss. Telegraph only. |
| `STRIKE` | 14 | Head lances forward at t=6. 100° front cone, 7.0 reach, venomous bite. |
| `SPIT` | 22 | Head jerks at t=10 and launches a `BasiliskVenomEntity` glob on an arc. |
| `TAIL_SWEEP` | 24 | Front coils, the tail whips a full arc, heavy knockback. |
| `BURROW_DOWN` | 26 | Head dives; segments hide progressively front to back. Dirt burst. |
| `BURROWED` | ≤ 70 | Travels underground toward the victim with `noPhysics`, untouchable, trailing block particles. |
| `BURROW_UP` | 22 | Erupts beneath the target; segments unhide back to front and launch victims. |
| `BLINDED` | latch | Head weaves side to side searching. Gaze disabled. |
| `DEATH` | 60 | Writhing collapse, coils going slack from head to tail. |

## The charged gaze

A per-player charge counter runs 0 → 50 ticks on the server. It rises only when
all of the following hold: the player is within 24 blocks, mutual line of sight,
the basilisk's head is aimed at the player, and the player's crosshair is within
about 12° of the basilisk's head specifically. It decays at twice that rate the
moment any condition breaks.

Client feedback rides on a new `basilisk_gaze` mob effect whose amplitude
carries the charge tier, 0 to 4. No custom packet is involved, so it behaves on
a dedicated server.

The overlay itself is drawn by `BasiliskGazeGuiMixin`, injected at the tail of
`Gui#render`, rather than by a Forge event. Forge 51 has no HUD hook at all:
both `RenderGuiEvent` and `RegisterGuiOverlaysEvent` were removed in 1.20.5 when
Mojang introduced the layered draw system, and Forge never replaced them. The
source files are still in the jar, commented out.

- Tier 0–1: faint grey desaturation at the screen edges, a low sub-bass swell.
- Tier 2–3: a stone-crackle texture creeps inward, the heartbeat rises in pitch.
- Tier 4: the vignette closes, the screen greys out, then petrification and
  death.

The player escapes by looking away, breaking line of sight, or blinding the
basilisk. The gaze cannot charge while the basilisk is blinded or burrowed.

## Blinded state

Damage landed while the attacker stands in the basilisk's front 60° cone
accumulates against a head-hitzone counter. Past 60 damage a permanent latch
trips for that individual: the eye cubes swap to a gouged texture on a second
render layer, the gaze goes dead, and targeting switches to hunting by sound —
the nearest entity that recently moved or attacked, rather than the nearest
visible one. Sprinting nearby becomes dangerous, sneaking becomes viable.

The latch is permanent by design. Blinding a basilisk is a real reward for a
real risk.

## Sounds

Eleven clips through the ElevenLabs SFX API, converted to mono OGG at 44.1 kHz
into `assets/hogwartsmagic/sounds/basilisk/`, generated by
`build_scripts/generate_basilisk_sounds.py`:

`ambient1` (deep slow hiss), `ambient2` (scales dragging over stone),
`hiss_threat`, `strike`, `spit`, `bite`, `burrow`, `erupt`, `gaze_heartbeat`,
`hurt`, `death`.

## Stats and spawning

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Hitbox | 3.5 W × 3.0 H |
| Max health | 300 |
| Movement speed | 0.30 |
| Attack damage | 18 |
| Follow range | 48 |
| Knockback resistance | 0.95 |

That box is what the basilisk *collides* with — it is used for movement and
pathing only, and sits over the front of the animal.

**Hits go to a row of eight `BasiliskPart` multipart hitboxes**, not to that box.
One axis-aligned volume cannot follow fifteen blocks of curving serpent, so the
parts are laid along the same path samples the model draws the spine from, which
means what you can see is what you can hit — round a corner, over a stair, and
up in the air when it rears. The parent overrides `isPickable()` to `false`, so
every blow arrives through a part.

| Part | Distance back | Size | Damage |
|---|---|---|---|
| head | −1.3 (ahead of origin) | 2.0 × 1.5 | ×1.30 |
| neck | 0.6 | 1.4 × 1.2 | ×1.00 |
| fore body | 2.2 | 1.8 × 1.5 | ×1.00 |
| mid body | 3.8 | 2.0 × 1.6 | ×1.00 |
| hind body | 5.4 | 1.8 × 1.5 | ×1.00 |
| fore tail | 7.4 | 1.4 × 1.2 | ×0.85 |
| tail | 9.6 | 1.1 × 0.9 | ×0.70 |
| tail tip | 12.0 | 0.8 × 0.7 | ×0.70 |

Parts are hit volumes only — they do not collide, so a passing tail shoves
nobody around, but it can be struck. Each is a little wider than the cubes it
covers, to absorb the lateral undulation the model layers over the path.

This also replaces the front-cone heuristic for blinding: damage that arrives
through the **head** part is what accumulates toward putting the eyes out, so
aiming at the head is now a real, readable choice. Damage that reaches the
parent directly (splash, magic, fall) still falls back to the cone test.

Part ids are pinned to `parentId + 1 + i` in the constructor. The server keys
its part lookup by the id each part happens to hold, the client rebuilds them
from the parent id in `recreateFromPacket`, and a disagreement means every blow
to the body silently lands on nothing.

The render bounding box is inflated to 20 blocks so the tail never culls.

Spawn egg only, as today. No natural spawns.

# Slytherin's Locket Cave & the Inferi — Design

Date: 2026-10-06
Status: approved in brainstorm, awaiting spec review

## The arc this belongs to

Voldemort's summoning is being replaced by a Horcrux hunt:

1. **Slytherin's Locket** — in a rare sea cave guarded by Inferi (**this spec**).
2. **Tom Riddle's Diary** — in the Chamber of Secrets beside the Basilisk (later spec).
3. **Hufflepuff's Cup** — in a special Gringotts vault (later spec, built last before the finale).
4. **Finale** — a player holding all three throws them into Fiendfyre together; with his anchors destroyed Voldemort is *forced out* and appears (later spec).

Arc rules fixed in the brainstorm:
- **The inventory is the progress.** No per-player Horcrux tracking. Whoever holds all three can end it; Horcruxes can be traded or stolen.
- Each Horcrux location holds **one** Horcrux that **never returns**, even after Voldemort dies. There are many such locations per world and they are very rare.
- Voldemort's Horcruxes are unrelated to the existing player Horcrux block (`HorcruxBlock`, `HorcruxData`, the respawn anchor). The new items never touch that code. They share one item tag, `hogwartsmagic:dark_lord_horcruxes`, which the finale checks.

The old Ward-triangle + Morsmordre ritual (`VoldemortSummoningEntity.tryStart`) stays in place until the finale spec replaces it.

## Scope of this spec

In: the cave structure, the Blood Seal, the chain and boat, the basin and Drink of Despair, Unquenchable Thirst, the ambush, the Inferius mob (model, animations, sounds, AI, natural spawning), Slytherin's Locket, Memory of the Cave and the Cave Map, Concealed Rock and its Revelio hook, the looted-cave tracker.

Out: the R.A.B. fake locket; the Diary, Cup and finale; any Horcrux destruction.

## 1. The cave structure

**Placement.** A jigsaw worldgen structure, `hogwartsmagic:locket_cave`, following the `chamber_hall` pattern. A jigsaw `Structure` places piece-by-piece per chunk, so the far-chunk `Feature` crash from the big-structure memory does not apply.
- Biomes: tag `hogwartsmagic:has_structure/locket_cave` = `minecraft:stony_shore`, `minecraft:beach`.
- Structure set: `random_spread`, spacing ≈ 160 chunks, separation ≈ 80 (about one per 2,500 blocks before biome filtering). Tune by playtest; the target is "very rare but findable with a map".
- Height: fixed so the template's waterline sits on sea level (y 62/63). The template brings its own headland, so it doesn't need a natural cliff.
- `spawn_overrides`: no natural monsters inside the piece box. Inferi in the cave come only from the cave's own logic.

**Template.** One NBT of about 64×64×40, generated by a Python script in `build_scripts/` (`generate_locket_cave.py`), as with the other structures. Palette: deepslate, blackstone, basalt, tuff; no light sources except the basin's glow. The lake floor is dark blocks, so the water reads as black.

**The route:**
1. **Cliff headland** with a sea-level crack plugged by **Concealed Rock** (§5).
2. **Swim tunnel:** about 20 blocks flooded, short enough to swim on one breath, rising into a dry antechamber.
3. **Blood Seal arch** (§2).
4. **The cavern:** a dome with a narrow ledge around a large black lake.
5. **The chain** at the shore, near the ledge's landing (§2).
6. **The island** at the centre: a small rock platform with the **basin** and a **crystal goblet** (decorative block beside it).

The structure is checked with the `rendering-minecraft-structures` skill before committing.

## 2. Blood Seal, chain and boat

**Blood Seal** (block, unbreakable in survival, explosion-proof). The arch is filled with these.
- Right-click from the outer side: the player takes **6 damage (3 hearts)** of a new damage type `hogwartsmagic:blood_toll` (bypasses armour, death message "paid the cave's toll"). The whole connected arch turns to an open state (no collision, invisible) for **60 s**, then reseals.
- From the inside it opens for free on right-click. Nobody can be trapped.
- It never reseals onto a player: if a player stands inside the arch, the reseal waits.

**Chain** (block, invisible and non-colliding until revealed). It's revealed by Revelio within range, or briefly when a player walks into its space, showing particles.
- Right-click the revealed chain: a **Cave Boat** rises at the shore if none is docked there.

**Cave Boat** (entity). One seat. Server-steered along a fixed path from the shore dock to the island dock and back (the path endpoints are stored as marker positions in the structure, read when the boat is summoned). Mounting starts the trip, and it stops at the far dock. It can't be broken, leashed or picked up, and it sinks away if left empty for 2 minutes. Extra passengers are refused ("The boat will carry only one").

## 3. Basin, Drink of Despair, thirst and ambush

**Basin** (block + block entity). The cave's state lives here:
- `FULL` → `DRINKING(drinker UUID, drinks 1–5, lastDrinkTick)` → `LOOTED` (permanent).
- Right-click by a player on the island while `FULL` or `DRINKING`, if they are the drinker or no one is: one drink.
- **6 drinks** empty it. Each drink applies an escalating set of effects (table below) plus a despair whisper in chat (a pool of lines, never quotes from the books).
- **Refill rule:** in `DRINKING`, if 30 s (600 ticks) pass without a drink, or the drinker leaves the island bounds, or the drinker dies, the state returns to `FULL`. The effects already taken stay.
- After drink 6, the basin shows the Locket. Right-clicking it gives the player **Slytherin's Locket**, applies **Unquenchable Thirst**, sets `LOOTED` and records the cave in the looted-cave tracker. The basin texture becomes dry.

| Drink | Effects added (refreshed) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Nausea 10 s |
| 2 | Nausea 15 s, Weakness I 30 s |
| 3 | + Slowness I 30 s, 2 damage |
| 4 | + Darkness 15 s, Slowness II, 3 damage |
| 5 | + Blindness 5 s, Mining Fatigue II 30 s, 4 damage |
| 6 | + Hunger II 30 s, 4 damage |

Drinks never kill on their own: drink damage is capped so the player stays at ≥1 health. Dying is still possible from the Thirst and the Inferi. Exact numbers are tunable constants in a pure `DespairRules` class.

**Unquenchable Thirst** (mob effect, harmful, hidden particles, 10 min duration).
- 1 damage every 100 ticks.
- Milk does **not** remove it (curative items cleared). Drinking a water bottle or potion has no effect on it; water from Aqua Eructo near the player vanishes in a puff, as in the lore.
- It's cured only by **drinking from the cave lake**: right-clicking lake water inside the cave's bounds with an empty hand or a glass bottle. This removes the effect and **starts the ambush**.

**Ambush** (run by the basin block entity as a server-side controller).
- About 16 Inferi rise from the lake in waves over 20 s (for example 4 every 5 s), spawning in water blocks around the island and climbing out.
- Ambush Inferi target the player who triggered it, then any player in the cavern.
- It ends when all ambush Inferi are dead or when no player is in the cave bounds. Survivors then walk back into the lake and despawn after about 2 min idle.
- **Warning grab:** whenever no ambush is running (any basin state, including `LOOTED`), entering the lake water summons 2–3 guardian Inferi, with a 60 s cooldown per cave. A looted cave never runs a full ambush again.

## 4. The Inferius

**Model and animations:** made in Blockbench with the Blockbench MCP pipeline (memory: `reference_blockbench_mcp_pipeline.md`). A gaunt, pale-grey humanoid, ribs showing, long dark wet hair, ragged cloth. Animations: `rise` (climbing out of water or ground), `walk` (slow shamble), `grab` (both arms forward, hold), `recoil` (flinching from fire), `idle_sway`. Blockbench MCP failed to connect in the brainstorm session; it must be running before modelling. Per the render-code memory, no switch on enums in the model or renderer.

**Sounds:** made with the ElevenLabs SFX pipeline (memory: `reference_elevenlabs_sfx.md`): wet rise, rasping breath ambient, grab hiss, hurt, death. Envelope-trimmed and level-matched.

**Stats and AI (`InferiusEntity`, extends `Monster`):**
- 24 health, slow (0.2 speed), armour 0, knockback resistance 0.4. It doesn't climb walls, can swim, and doesn't drown.
- **Damage reduction:** takes 30% of damage that isn't fire-type (`#minecraft:is_fire` damage types, plus the mod's fire spells' damage sources). It catches fire from any fire source, and takes **2×** damage from all sources while burning.
- **Grab attack:** on melee hit, pins the target for 40 ticks (strong movement root via the mod's existing crowd-control path, `combat/CrowdControl`, so diminishing returns apply). If the Inferius or target is in or touching water, it pulls the target 1 block toward deeper water per second during the grab, instead of rooting. Grab cooldown 5 s per Inferius.
- **Fire fear:** it flees (avoid goal, about 6 blocks) from players holding a lit fire source, active Incendio or Confringo impacts within 4 blocks in the last 3 s, fire blocks and lava.
- It doesn't burn in daylight (it isn't undead in the vanilla sense), but natural spawns obey monster light rules.

**Natural spawning:** category `MONSTER`, rare weight, night only via light rules, biomes `hogwartsmagic:dementor_fen` and `#minecraft:is_swamp` (or vanilla swamp and mangrove swamp). Drops: rotten flesh 0–2, bone 0–1. It never drops anything Horcrux-related. Cave Inferi drop nothing.

## 5. Items, finding the cave, Revelio

**Slytherin's Locket** (item): stack size 1, `fireResistant`, immune to explosions and cactus, never despawns as an item entity, Epic rarity. While it's in a player's inventory it sends a whisper line in chat at random every few minutes (flavour only, no stat effects). Tag `hogwartsmagic:dark_lord_horcruxes`. Has a lore tooltip.

**Memory of the Cave** (item, a vial):
- Loot: about 15% chance in Death Eater camp chests (loot modifier or table edit); about 2% drop from `WizardEntity` (the Death Eaters), plus a small Looting bonus.
- Use (drink animation, 32 ticks): plays a short vision (screen tint overlay plus 2–3 chat lines describing a cliff above a black sea), then turns into a **Cave Map**. Done server-side; the vision overlay is a client-only effect triggered by packet.
- **Cave Map:** a vanilla filled map with a destination decoration on the nearest **unlooted** locket cave within a 100-chunk search, built like the vanilla explorer map. If none is found: "The memory is too faint" and the vial is not consumed.
- Unlooted search: the looted-cave tracker is a `SavedData` set of structure start positions; the search walks candidate starts nearest-first and skips looted ones. The search runs only on item use, never on a tick path (big-structure memory: no structure lookups on ticks).

**Concealed Rock** (block): looks like the cave's stone and can be mined normally. Revelio cast within 48 blocks makes all Concealed Rock in range glow (particles, client) for 30 s and turn passable and invisible for 60 s, then return. Hooked into `RevelioSpell.revealHidden`. Revelio also reveals the chain (§2) when cast inside the cavern.

## 6. Server/client safety

All state changes are server-side: basin, seal, chain, boat steering, effects, ambush, map creation, tracker. The client only renders (Inferius model, basin glow, seal, Concealed Rock glow, vision overlay), and gets vision and glow triggers via packets in `network/SpellPackets` or a new small packet class. No client classes are referenced from common code (the `ClientSideBridge` pattern). Avoid entity event bytes 21, 35 and 63 (memory).

## 7. Testing

- **Unit tests** (pure rule classes): `DespairRules` (drink escalation, damage cap, refill timeout and island-leave reset), `AmbushSchedule` (wave timing and counts, end conditions), `InferiusDamage` (fire vs non-fire, burning multiplier), and the looted-cave search skip logic.
- **`runServer` data check** for the registries, tags, loot, worldgen JSON and structure set (memory: the cheapest full data check).
- **Structure render check** of `locket_cave.nbt` before committing.
- **Client playthrough:** find a cave with a map, Revelio the entrance, pay the toll, chain, boat, 6 drinks, refill-on-timeout check, thirst, ambush, escape, looted state, and a second map skipping the looted cave. Wild Inferi at night in the fen.

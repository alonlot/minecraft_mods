Hogwarts Assignments
Earn the spell, or find the book

Homework that ends in a spellbook.

Some chests hold a sealed scroll in place of a spellbook. Finish the task written on it and the scroll turns into the book it promised. The harder the spell, the longer the task.

How it works

From scroll to spellbook

  1. Find it. Open a chest. Where a spellbook could be, a scroll can be instead.
  2. Accept it. Right-click the scroll. It is now bound to you, and nobody else can use it.
  3. Do the task. Right-click again to read the parchment. The action bar shows each step as you make progress.
  4. Claim the book. When chat says it is complete, right-click the scroll. It turns into the spellbook. Items the task asks you to bring are taken at that moment.

Task size by rarity

No task needs the spell it teaches. Some tasks need another spell first, for example Stupefy needs Expelliarmus, and that spell is never rarer than the one it unlocks.
